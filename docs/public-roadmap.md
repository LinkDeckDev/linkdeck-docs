# LinkDeck Public Roadmap

This roadmap describes the public development direction for LinkDeck.

It intentionally stays at product and milestone level. Production-critical engineering, supplier data, manufacturing details and security-sensitive implementation remain private.

> LinkDeck is under active development. Priorities, specifications and timing may change as the physical prototype is validated.

## Current phase — Digital preparation

**Status: In progress**

Before hardware validation begins, the current focus is on:

- Maintaining the central project documentation
- Preparing public documentation and repositories
- Completing UI/UX planning for the 280 × 456 display
- Preparing the prototype test and acceptance process
- Preparing content and devlog workflows
- Keeping a clear boundary between public and private engineering material

The six planned software expansion features remain queued until this base work is complete.

## Next milestone — Physical prototype validation

**Status: Waiting for hardware**

When the first prototype components and enclosure are available, the project will validate:

- Enclosure dimensions, fit and print quality
- Board placement and USB-C access
- NFC placement
- Mechanical closure
- Real prototype photography
- General usability of the physical form factor

## Functional prototype

**Status: Planned**

After the mechanical baseline is validated, the next goal is a functional handheld prototype with:

- Display and touch bring-up
- First functional UI
- Profile navigation
- NFC sharing flow
- QR fallback
- Real power-consumption measurements
- Battery selection based on measured requirements

## Product refinement

**Status: Planned**

Once the functional prototype is proven, work can move toward:

- Refining the enclosure from real-world feedback
- Freezing the product architecture
- Improving usability and reliability
- Preparing the product for repeatable assembly and validation
- Building the public documentation required for users and developers

## Launch preparation

**Status: Future**

Crowdfunding and launch work remains paused until the product has a validated physical prototype and credible production assumptions.

Before any campaign or launch is considered ready, LinkDeck should have:

- A functional physical prototype
- Demonstrated NFC and UI flows
- Measured power requirements
- A selected battery
- Credible production and shipping assumptions
- Real product photography/video
- A validated product story and pricing direction

## Software priorities

After the core UI/UX and digital foundation are stable, the planned software expansion order is:

1. Multi-profile + Quick Share
2. Dynamic QR
3. Web Configuration Panel
4. PIN / Private Profiles
5. OTA Firmware Updates
6. Themes

These are product priorities, not promises of immediate availability.

## Development principle

**Validate before promising.**

Physical measurements and working prototypes take priority over speculative specifications, launch dates or feature claims.
