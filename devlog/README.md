## Publication policy

Public devlogs may discuss the development process, lessons learned, prototype milestones and selected technical decisions.

They must not expose production CAD, complete firmware, manufacturing data, credentials, provisioning internals or other security-sensitive material.

Capabilities are described as working only after physical validation. Before validation, wording should clearly distinguish targets, plans and prototype directions from confirmed results.
